About

Machines, Meaning, and Material

Blue Suitcase is a digital arts studio focused on the intersections of art, design, and emerging technologies. Our studio creates installations, visuals, and interactive systems to translate the language of technology into tangible, expressive forms.

We are drawn to infrastructure that shapes how we communicate, move, and make meaning of the world. Through research, material experimentation, and collaboration, we create work that reflects on the sensory impact of connected life. Whether we're working with code, circuitry, printed matter, or spatial forms, we treat technology not just as a tool but as a subject: something to be questioned, abstracted, and reimagined.

Precedent

Interfacing with the World

We're inspired by early experiments in telematic art, works that collapse space and time through networks, transmission, and real-time connection. It celebrates the playful potential of technology to surprise, connect, and disrupt everyday life, revealing new possibilities for community, presence, and participation.
